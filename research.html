How Research Works

A transparent process from invitation to completion.

STEP 01

Explore a Project

Review the research purpose, eligibility requirements, privacy terms and participation instructions.

STEP 02

Share Your Response

Provide thoughtful, truthful answers based on your actual experience. Never submit fabricated feedback.

STEP 03

Review & Verification

Submitted research may be checked for completeness and quality according to the project's stated rules.

Our Research Principles

  • Honest opinions and genuine experiences only.
  • No fabricated reviews, ratings or endorsements.
  • Clear project instructions and eligibility criteria.
  • Personal information should be handled according to the published privacy policy.
  • No guaranteed income or guaranteed availability of research projects.
