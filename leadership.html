What Matters in Leadership

A responsible leadership framework should prioritize quality over recruitment or deposits.

Research Performance

Accuracy, completeness, genuine feedback and adherence to research instructions.

Helpful coordination, clear communication and support for ethical research practices.

Consistency

Reliable participation and sustained quality across eligible research projects.

Integrity

Respect for privacy, honest reporting and zero tolerance for fabricated reviews.
